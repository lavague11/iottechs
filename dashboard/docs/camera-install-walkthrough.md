# IOT TECHS Camera Installation Walkthrough

The job checklist a technician fills out on every install. It follows the same steps and order as the Security Camera Installation & Training Manual.

## 01 Site walk and survey

Look for: building edges, parking lot and entrances to orient the survey; NVR spot; power; network; longest and shared cable routes; drilling surfaces; hazards.

Do: Hold the phone flat and rotate the survey until the curb and building match what you see. Check a second landmark. Confirm camera count, names and locations against the work order.

- [ ] Survey oriented using two landmarks
- [ ] Camera count and locations confirmed (changes: \_\_\_\_\_\_)
- [ ] NVR location: ventilation, mounting and cable entry suitable
- [ ] Two permanent outlets near the NVR (NVR + display); third if a Wi-Fi extender is planned
- [ ] Router or wired network near the NVR (if not, LAN route: \_\_\_\_\_\_)
- [ ] Extension cord needed for temporary setup only
- [ ] Longest and shared routes identified
- [ ] Drilling surfaces noted: wood / drywall / brick / concrete / metal
- [ ] Hazards, hidden utilities or approvals needed: \_\_\_\_\_\_
- [ ] Before photos taken of each camera and NVR location

## 02 NVR and hard drive

Look for: mounting spot, ventilation, permanent outlet, LAN and cable entry.

Do: With power disconnected, install the hard drive. Mount the NVR, connect HDMI, LAN and power. Power up, get it online, initialize the drive and set time and recording. Extension cords are for approved temporary setup only.

Tools: drill/driver, Phillips bit, small screwdriver set, monitor, HDMI cable, mouse, phone with camera app, Fastback knife.

Materials: NVR, approved hard drive, mounting hardware, HDMI cable, LAN patch cable, extension cord (temporary).

- [ ] Hard drive installed with power off
- [ ] NVR mounted and connected
- [ ] NVR online
- [ ] Hard drive recognized, status Normal
- [ ] QR / app enrollment done if assigned
- [ ] Photo of NVR and drive status uploaded

## 03 Drill cable paths

Look for: wood, drywall, metal, brick or concrete; hidden pipes and wires; fire-rated walls.

Do: Pick the right drill and bit. Check for hidden utilities first. Mark and drill every approved hole in one pass. Sleeve, grommet and seal penetrations. If anything is uncertain or unsafe, stop and call the PM.

Tools: impact driver, hammer drill, SDS drill, drill bits (wood, masonry, metal), stud and wire detector, safety glasses, gloves, M12 vacuum, ladder.

Materials: sleeves, grommets, anchors, exterior sealant.

- [ ] Hidden utilities checked before drilling
- [ ] All holes drilled in one batch
- [ ] Exterior holes sealed
- [ ] Dust cleaned up
- [ ] Photo of each exterior entry

## 04 Run Cat6 drops

Look for: longest routes, shared paths, obstacles, support points, service-loop spots.

Do: Start at the NVR. Pull the longest shared runs first, several cables together. Split only at each branch. Leave service slack at both ends. No kinks, crushing or over-pulling. Ceiling tiles and grid are not cable supports.

Tools: fish poles, fish tape, pull string, cable cutter, ladder, Fastback knife.

Materials: Cat6 cable, pull string, zip ties, single clips, double clips, electrical tape.

- [ ] Longest shared runs pulled first
- [ ] Service slack left at NVR and camera ends
- [ ] Every cable labeled at both ends
- [ ] Photo of each run before it's closed up

## 05 NVR-side terminations

Look for: which cable feeds which camera, and its NVR or PoE port.

Do: Strip, terminate, label and test every NVR-side end before moving out to the cameras.

Tools: cable stripper, pass-through RJ45 crimper, Scout Pro 3 PoE tester + remote, toner + wand.

Materials: pass-through RJ45 connectors, cable labels, patch cables.

- [ ] Every NVR-side end terminated and labeled
- [ ] Every cable tested: no opens, shorts, reversals or split pairs
- [ ] Cable-to-port map noted in the work order

## 06 Cable management

Look for: loose loops, exposed cable, sharp edges, untidy bundles, unsupported spans.

Do: Secure and organize every run with approved supports. Don't pinch or overtighten Cat6. Size clips and staples to the bundle. Never fasten to sprinkler pipes, electrical conduit or ceiling grid. Neat, straight, level.

Tools: cable cutter, impact driver, low-voltage stapler, ladder.

Materials: single clips, double clips, 3/4-inch clips, zip ties, mounting zip ties, Velcro straps, low-voltage staples, cable labels.

- [ ] Every run supported, neat and straight
- [ ] No crushed or overtightened cable
- [ ] Photo of finished cable runs

## 07 Mount and verify each camera

Look for: mount surface, view, and connection method (connector pocket hole, junction box or external route).

Do: Terminate the camera end and test the cable before mounting. Conceal the RJ45 in an approved pocket hole, or protect it with a junction box. Mount with three correct screws, anchors where needed. Seal the outdoor connector. Aim and confirm live view.

Tools: drill/driver, Phillips bit, marker, level, cable stripper, pass-through RJ45 crimper, Scout Pro 3 PoE tester, ladder.

Materials: camera, pass-through RJ45 connectors, weatherproof RJ45 boot, junction box, anchors and screws, exterior sealant, electrical tape (backup wrap only).

- [ ] Camera end tested before mounting
- [ ] Mounted firm and level
- [ ] Outdoor connector sealed
- [ ] Live image confirmed and aimed
- [ ] Photo uploaded under that camera's name in the work order

## 08 Final NVR inspection

Look for: offline cameras, wrong angles or names, recording faults, anything flagged earlier.

Do: Go back to the NVR. Confirm every camera is online and aimed right, the drive says Normal, and recording plays back.

Tools: monitor, mouse, phone, Scout Pro 3 PoE tester.

Materials: issue list.

- [ ] Every camera live on the monitor
- [ ] NVR online
- [ ] Hard drive status Normal
- [ ] One instant replay played back
- [ ] Remaining failures written up for the PM

## 09 Clean, return and PM closeout

Look for: trash, cable scraps, tie tails, drill dust, tools left behind, unfinished work.

Do: Clean the site and replace ceiling tiles. Check every tool back against the load-out table. Take completion photos. Mark the job ready and notify the PM. The PM handles channel naming, the customer app and handoff.

Tools: M12 vacuum, cable cutter, phone, tool load-out table.

Materials: trash bags, cleaning supplies.

- [ ] Site clean, ceiling tiles replaced
- [ ] Every tool checked back in
- [ ] Completion photos uploaded
- [ ] Exceptions marked in the work order
- [ ] PM notified
