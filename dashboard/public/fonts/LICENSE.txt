WHY THESE ARE SELF-HOSTED
-------------------------
They were loaded from fonts.googleapis.com, which cost two third-party DNS
lookups plus two TLS handshakes before a single glyph could be requested, and
the stylesheet was render-blocking: roughly 2.1s of the home page's 3.1s LCP.
Served from our own origin on the already-open connection, that cost disappears.

Both families are VARIABLE fonts, so one file per subset covers every weight the
site uses (400-800) — replacing the four to five static weights the Google CSS
used to request.

These files came from Google Fonts' own CDN (fonts.gstatic.com) and are the
same binaries it serves; see dashboard/app/globals.css and public/home.html for
the @font-face declarations that reference them.
